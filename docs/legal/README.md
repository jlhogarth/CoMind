## Activation procedure

1. Joseph and counsel resolve the rights-holder and human-authorship questions in the review register. Preserve signed assignments and sensitive evidence in restricted storage, not this public repository.
2. Counsel reviews the agreement, acceptable-use terms, remedies, jurisdiction, privacy duties, applicable statutory exceptions and prior grants. Record the approved document versions and hashes.
3. For each recipient, execute a separate authorization identifying both legal parties and authorized signatories, material/version, named users, permitted purpose and environment, start and end dates, fees if any, notices, governing law and dispute forum. Identify each expressly permitted exception. Without all required fields and both parties' acceptance, no permission is granted by this package.
4. Record assent through a signed instrument or a counsel-approved affirmative acceptance mechanism. Merely posting terms, issuing an invitation, merging a PR or supplying a URL does not execute an agreement.
5. Grant the minimum access required; preserve acceptance and access records privately. Keep source access separate from production, credentials, customer data and spending authority.
6. Before expiry or termination, review access and follow the agreement's return, deletion and evidence-preservation terms. Do not delete evidence to enforce confidentiality.

## Operational protection

Keep proprietary implementation and unpublished design material in access-controlled repositories. Publish only intentionally approved demonstrations and descriptions. A visibility change requires the development contract's Forward Impact Gate, including CI costs, Pages, forks, integrations and recovery consequences. This PR does not change visibility.

Retain development chronology and human-authored contributions. Assess copyright registration and any patent filing with counsel. Do not publish a detailed inventory of secret mechanisms, credentials, customer information or proprietary enforcement internals in an IP register. Public availability cannot be undone by adding a confidentiality heading.

Contributions require provenance and appropriate written rights before acceptance. Neither this package nor an ordinary PR silently assigns a contributor's ownership. Do not characterize AI output as human authorship merely because a human supplied prompts or approved a merge.

## Maintenance

Joseph is accountable for licensing decisions; counsel is responsible for legal review when engaged. Every substantive terms change gets a version, rationale and review record. Executed agreements continue under their accepted versions unless validly amended. Keep signed agreements, recipient identities and detailed exposure evidence in restricted storage. This directory is not an executed-agreement database.
