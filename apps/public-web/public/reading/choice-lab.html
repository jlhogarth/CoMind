Choice Lab

Compare two options without pretending that a weighted score is the truth.

Private, browser-only educational exercise. No information is transmitted or stored. The comparison is based solely on what you enter, not independently verified facts.

1. Define your alternatives

For each criterion, choose its importance (1 to 5) and rate each option (1 to 5). A rating of 0 means unknown and does not count as a zero-quality outcome.

2. Examine the comparison

What the numbers cannot tell you

    Before you decide

    Check the highest-impact uncertain criterion against a primary source. Ask what evidence would reverse your preference, whether the decision is reversible, and who has authority to act. A score never overrides these checks.

    3. Investigate rather than guess

    Write down the source and date behind each rating. If two alternatives are close, the next useful action is usually a targeted test, not greater numerical precision.

    For a structured evidence review, continue to the Decision Clinic or the reasoning field guide.
