Confidential Information means nonpublic information disclosed under the authorization that is marked confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential given its nature and circumstances. Licensee must comply with the incorporated Acceptable Use terms, restrict access to named users, use individual accounts and available multifactor authentication, protect stored and transmitted confidential data, and keep credentials out of source code and logs. No production or personal-data processing is authorized merely by this agreement. Notify Licensor without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after discovering unauthorized access to or disclosure of Materials, with facts then known and subsequent updates. Cooperate reasonably in containment and preservation of evidence. Do not conceal or destroy incident records.

On reasonable written request, provide an accurate compliance certification and records limited to the authorized use. This clause grants no remote access, hidden monitoring or unilateral inspection right. Evidence collection must comply with privacy and other applicable law.

## 7. Suspension and termination

The license expires at the end of the agreed term. Licensor may suspend access immediately when reasonably necessary to contain suspected unauthorized disclosure or material misuse, providing notice and reasons where lawful. Other termination rights must be stated in the authorization.

Upon expiry or termination, cease licensed use and, within ten calendar days, return or delete Materials and Confidential Information and certify completion. Legally required evidence and inaccessible routine backups may be retained only as required by law or normal secure retention schedules, with continued confidentiality, no operational reuse and deletion when retention ends. Do not erase litigation holds or incident evidence. Accrued rights, ownership, confidentiality, use restrictions on retained material, remedies and applicable dispute terms survive to the extent appropriate. Termination does not extinguish independently granted third-party rights.
